鳥居 Torii

Coming soon

A torii marks a threshold. It is not the destination — it is the gate you pass through to reach it.

Financial systems are guarded by vocabulary. A term you do not know is enough to stop a sentence, and enough of them will stop a reader altogether. This face is the way in: the concepts, the institutions, and the sources, explained for someone who wants to read the record themselves.

The point is not to hand over conclusions. It is to make the material readable.
