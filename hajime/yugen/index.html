幽玄 Yūgen

Public money

Yūgen is depth that shows without being fully seen. This face takes what is meant to be visible and often is not: budgets against actual spending, and whether the record matches the claim.

This is not a watchdog. We are not hunting for wrongdoing — we are putting figures that are already public next to each other so their shape becomes apparent. Where the published intention and the published outcome disagree, we say so and show both. What that means is for the reader to judge.
