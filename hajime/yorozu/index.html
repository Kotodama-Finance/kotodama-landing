万 Yorozu

Sector by sector

Yorozu is the ten thousand things — the whole seen as its parts. This face is the index of sectors, not a sector: insurance, banking, capital markets, real estate, and whatever follows.

Each one gets the same treatment: how it is regulated, who operates in it, and what its numbers say. A sector becomes legible once you know the rules it runs under and the shape of who is inside it. The figures come after that, and mean less without it.
