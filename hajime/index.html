肇 Hajime

Data and analysis

First analysis on the way

Hajime is the first stroke — the beginning of a thing. It is where the platform starts, because reading a financial system has to start with the record: what was published, by whom, and when.

This face holds three lines of work. Taichi takes the whole: the macro picture, the aggregates, the movements that set the conditions everything else operates under. Yorozu takes the parts: sector by sector, how each one is regulated, who operates in it, and what the numbers say. Yūgen takes what is meant to be visible and often is not: public money, and whether the record matches the claim.

  • 太一 Taichi The macro picture
  • 万 Yorozu Sector by sector
  • 幽玄 Yūgen Public money

Whatever is published here will carry its sources with it — every figure traceable to the document it came from.
