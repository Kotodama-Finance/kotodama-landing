太一 Taichi

The macro picture

Taichi is the great one — the whole before it divides into parts. This face takes an economy at that level: the aggregates, the flows between sectors, and the policy decisions that set the conditions everything else operates under.

Money supply, public debt, national accounts, the movements of a central bank. None of it explains a single company, and all of it explains the room a company has to move in. The parts only mean something against the whole.
