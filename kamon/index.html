家紋 Kamon

Community and network

Coming soon

A kamon is a family crest: the mark that shows what you belong to. Small, specific, and worn where others can see it.

Understanding a financial system is not solitary work. People who read the same sources notice different things in them, and the ones already inside an industry know what its published numbers leave out. This face is meant to be where that gets said out loud — a place to compare readings, argue about them, and find the people worth arguing with.

What it becomes depends on who turns up.
