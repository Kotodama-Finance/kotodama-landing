渡世 Tosei

Personal finance in Japan

Coming soon

Tosei is making your way through the world — the ordinary, practical business of getting by. Living in a country means dealing with its financial system whether or not you ever intended to.

Japan’s is dense and mostly documented in Japanese: pensions and social insurance, the tax year, NISA and iDeCo, what changes when you are a resident and what changes when you are not. Most of it is knowable. Little of it is gathered anywhere in one place.

This face is for anyone living here who has run into that — residents and newcomers alike.
