About

Capital moves in currents most people never see. Kotodama Finance exists to make those currents visible — and to keep them open to everyone.

The cube is our map. Six faces, held at the same level, because understanding a financial system means holding data, people, learning, and action together — not ranking one over the rest.

Japan is the first node, not the frontier. We start here because it is where we live. The method is meant to travel.

Each face carries a Japanese word — 肇, 素顔, 絆 — chosen because it names what that area does more precisely than an English label would. The words are not decoration; where they come from is a longer story. What they promise is written plainly, in language anyone can read.

Method

Everything here traces back to the source.

Not summaries of summaries. We read the filings, releases, and datasets themselves, cite where every figure came from, and publish the method alongside the finding. This is the moat — and the reason to trust the map.

Primary source

Every claim starts at the origin document — a BOJ release, an MOF table, a JPX disclosure — never a second-hand retelling.

Traceability

Each number carries its origin, its date, and its revision. Follow any figure back to the exact release it came from.

Transparency

The methodology is published, the access is free, and there are no paywalls. If it cannot be shown openly, it does not ship.

How we work

  1. 01Collect the primary release the moment it publishes.
  2. 02Verify figures against the issuing body’s own tables.
  3. 03Cite the origin, date, and revision on every number.
  4. 04Publish the analysis and its method, openly and for free.
